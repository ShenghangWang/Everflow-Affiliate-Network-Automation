## Ranked findings

### CR-01 — High — AM and Traffic Guard need shared ordering and supersession for the same target

**Artifact locations:** `architecture.md:74–80` (A04 action and outcome cycle), `architecture.md:94–100` (A06 mitigation and recovery), `architecture.md:114–115` (authorization and outbox), `architecture.md:129–132` (bounded writes and invalidation), `architecture.md:158–162` (claims/recovery).

**Source locations:** `evidence/intake/roles-original.txt:64–78` (R p.2, pause/start/cap requests), `evidence/intake/roles-original.txt:99–108` (R p.3, stop-loss and reroute), `evidence/intake/roles-original.txt:151–162` (R p.4, wrong cap/payout and wasted-spend redlines). The adopted brief requires specific AM/Ops authority and failure paths at `evidence/intake/delivery-brief-v1.3.md:113–119` and `:199–205`.

**Concrete failure:** An AM resume/reroute request for campaign C is queued or awaiting approval. Traffic Guard subsequently discovers a broken destination, pauses C, receives a successful readback, and opens an incident. The older AM action then becomes eligible and executes. Its target, content, policy version and expiry may still be valid, yet it undoes the stop-loss measure while the incident remains open. A single worker prevents simultaneous transport calls but does not establish causal order between different valid intents. Unique intent keys prevent duplicate intents, not conflicting ones.

**Evidence / uncertainty:** The HLD requires bounds, preconditions, readback and rollback. It does not define a shared per-target control version, emergency hold, supersession rule, or which owner wins between A04 and A06. No implementation was inspected; this is an omitted cross-role invariant rather than evidence that a particular queue currently misorders actions. Generic policy revalidation could implement the remedy, but the required policy behavior is not stated.

**Minimal remedy:** Give each controlled account/campaign/route a control epoch or equivalent version. Bind a consequential command to that version and its expected preconditions. A mitigation decision advances the version, places an incident hold and invalidates incompatible queued actions. Serialize writes per target. Resume or rollback during an open incident must use fresh observed state and an explicitly authorized recovery decision; it must not revive an older command automatically. State which Ops/account owner may clear the hold and how external/manual changes are reconciled.

**Tradeoff:** More conservative invalidation creates additional approvals and can delay legitimate recovery. It is justified for shared targets with pause/resume/reroute effects; do not impose this machinery on harmless drafts.

**Falsifiable verification:** In a fixture adapter, queue an authorized AM resume, perform a newer Guard pause, then make the older resume due and restart the worker. The old resume must remain superseded/blocked and the campaign paused. Also race two actors, inject a manual external change, and test a separately approved recovery against the new version. Every command must have an attributable winning decision and verified outcome; an old lease/receipt must not overwrite it. These tests are proposed, not executed in this review.

### CR-02 — Medium — Signature acceptance and business activation are not consistently separated

**Artifact locations:** `architecture.md:54–60` (A02 transitions and acceptance, especially “signature fixture activates exactly one account”), `architecture.md:66–70` (A03 release/activation and failed setup), `architecture.md:76–80` (AM activation follow-through), `architecture.md:110–111` (onboarding and mapping ownership).

**Source locations:** `evidence/intake/roles-original.txt:33–45` (R p.1, signed contract leads to customer master/group), `evidence/intake/roles-original.txt:50–63` (R p.2, integration configuration and tests). The adopted brief separately requires onboarding activation ownership and technical release at `evidence/intake/delivery-brief-v1.3.md:66–67`.

**Concrete failure:** The signature webhook passes its fixture acceptance and marks a partner active even though the required postback mapping is incomplete or sandbox checks failed. Recurring AM then treats the partner as activated and starts expected-activity checks; routing/revenue systems may likewise see an active account. A03 says failed technical setup must not mark onboarding complete, but A02's stated acceptance permits interpreting a signature as the activation event.

**Evidence / uncertainty:** A02 includes an activation checklist, so the intended implementation may already require technical readiness. The acceptance scenario does not state that prerequisite, and there is no common readiness predicate linking the two cases. This is a concrete ambiguity/inconsistency in the acceptance contract, not proof that signed-only activation exists in code. The original source promises master/group creation after signing; it does not establish that all technical activation gates have passed.

**Minimal remedy:** Separate `signed/account-created`, `technical-ready`, `activation-approved` and `first-outcome-verified` as needed. Define one shared activation predicate over the required checklist, applicable integration release/test version, and accountable owner. An integration-free account may explicitly mark the integration gate not applicable. Change A02's acceptance to say a signature creates exactly one account **pending activation**, unless the complete readiness predicate is already satisfied. Keep initial activation distinct from the first accepted outcome checked by AM.

**Tradeoff:** An additional readiness decision can delay activation and requires a deliberate exception path. It prevents a webhook from implicitly granting operational readiness and keeps exceptions visible.

**Falsifiable verification:** Deliver duplicate and out-of-order signature events while the mapping is missing or a sandbox test has failed. Exactly one account may exist, but it must remain pending activation with an owner and blocker. Supply the approved matching mapping/test version and complete the checklist; activate once, then separately verify the first agreed outcome. A later material mapping failure/change must reopen the relevant readiness issue. No such test result is claimed here.

### CR-03 — Medium — Fetching all pages does not establish a complete snapshot of a changing report

**Artifact locations:** `architecture.md:84–90` (reconciliation), `architecture.md:94–100` (Guard completeness gate), `architecture.md:112` and `:120` (snapshot revision, watermark, pagination and corrections), `architecture.md:170` (page-based conversion-report candidate).

**Source locations:** `evidence/intake/business-original.txt:22` (B P035, click/conversion anomalies), `evidence/intake/roles-original.txt:79–93` (R p.2, report comparison and evidence), `evidence/intake/roles-original.txt:99–108` (R p.3, mitigation). The adopted brief requires explicit lateness/corrections and freshness at `evidence/intake/delivery-brief-v1.3.md:135–143`.

**Concrete failure:** Page 1 is fetched. Before page 2 is fetched, a conversion is rejected, corrected, inserted, or moves in the report's sort order. Offset/page-based results can now repeat an item and omit another. All pages were committed, so the proposed watermark condition passes, but the assembled snapshot is not a consistent report. A05 can produce a false discrepancy; A06 can regard incomplete counts as fresh/complete and propose mitigation. Overlap and later closed-window reconciliation may repair history after the initial decision, but do not make that earlier snapshot complete.

**Evidence / uncertainty:** The packet correctly leaves actual tenant pagination/freshness unverified and requires all pages before advancing a watermark. It does not specify a snapshot-consistency or control-total test for mutable pages. The selected vendor's exact sort, snapshot and mutation behavior is unknown; I am not asserting that its API necessarily exhibits this scenario. The defect is the missing acquisition-completeness contract for an input used by the safety gate.

**Minimal remedy:** Add report-attempt consistency evidence: fixed declared window/as-of point, stable ordering/cursor or vendor snapshot token where supported, unique-ID checks, and authoritative counts/totals where available. If the vendor supplies no consistent-read guarantee, use an owner-approved settling/reconciliation procedure and explicitly mark unproven snapshots partial/uncertain. Disable automated mitigation and final reconciliation conclusions for such inputs; keep the owned data-health case open. Do not infer completeness solely from reaching the final page. Define what corrections invalidate a previously accepted comparison/decision.

**Tradeoff:** Stronger consistency can increase calls and delay freshness, especially against shared tenant quotas. A deliberate freshness/completeness tradeoff with alert-only behavior is safer than silently labeling a moving report complete.

**Falsifiable verification:** Use a fake paginated source that inserts, removes and corrects records between page requests, including beyond the initial overlap period. The result must either match an authoritative immutable snapshot/control total or remain visibly uncertain; it must not trigger an automatic mitigation/final variance result. A later corrected complete snapshot must version/reopen the affected outcome without double-counting. Also verify page-boundary, DST and currency/status cases against the eventual permitted tenant contract. These checks were not run here.

## Important strengths and challenges that did not become defects

- **Source fidelity and scope:** The three provided source documents and explicit employer directions govern the assignment. The exact F `(1)` filename is retained without assuming equivalence to the unsuffixed brief name. The six-role tables, numerical aspirations, HR/compensation context and factory ambitions all receive dispositions (`requirements.md:86–141`). It is reasonable to preserve the compensation material as context rather than add payroll to the prototype. No inferred insurance employer business fact, insurance-only core field or displaced mandate was found. The generic baseline and removable optional example are explicit (`requirements.md:145–147`; `architecture.md:5`).
- **Authority and money:** The system distinguishes human approval from delegated policy, binds versions and recipients, invalidates material edits, keeps finance calculations deterministic, and treats collection/contract output as drafts absent authority. Exact semantic prose validation still needs the held-out/model checks; the packet does not claim that valid JSON alone makes a message commercially correct.
- **Reliability boundary:** Transactional inbox/outbox, durable action states, leases, finite retries and unknown-outcome quarantine are appropriate. The HLD explicitly acknowledges the reply-after-final-check/external ingestion gap and refuses exactly-once claims from a local ID (`architecture.md:158–162`). Eventual provider lookup must be tested for whether negative results actually prove nonacceptance; otherwise the stated quarantine rule should prevail. I do not treat a documented residual race or an unverified provider contract as an implemented defect.
- **Bounded AI and security:** Two total attempts/event, a common inference boundary, token/deadline/monetary ceilings, worst-case reservations and uncertain timeout usage address the factory's unbounded-cost claims. Model outage preserves owned work while deterministic calculation/scheduling continues (`architecture.md:197–205`). Prompt injection is constrained by withholding tools/credentials and using typed commands, rather than relying only on instructions. The 12–20-case evaluation is an initial quality check, not proof of production accuracy. Actual provider token/reasoning caps, prices and language accuracy remain to be demonstrated.
- **Honest integration assumptions:** The Network conversion report is a documented candidate, not a guessed universal tenant endpoint. Other operations remain abstract where access/schema is unknown. Channel initiation, identity, suppression, replies and limits are distinguished; Skype is excluded and Telegram is not treated as arbitrary cold outreach. No live tenant/model/channel result is fabricated.
- **Daily delivery:** `README.md:25–31` and the sponsor clarification treat daily minimums as floors, explicitly allow later work to move forward, and preserve a verified demo revision. I found no artificial calendar barrier. A useful dependency-free continuation is the connected generic BD path and its principal recovery checks, then the two-source dedupe/outcome replay or fixture discrepancy-to-owned-closure increment. Both add accepted outcomes and evidence without waiting for credentials. Pending Gemini does not justify idle time or a false completed-review claim. The three-day local path is plausible after installed dependencies; a successful launch, exercised UI and relevant T01–T12 evidence are still required to substantiate it.
- **Acquisition resilience:** `architecture.md:209–232` separates dated research observations, retail-specific evidence, causal limits, hypotheses and proposed responses. No employer channel mix is invented, no Adobe retail uplift is converted into an affiliate forecast, and no architecture guarantees survival. Replaceable inputs, retained unknown attribution, source concentration, contribution completeness, retention and provider-outage behavior are practical responses. The proposed source-shock replay uses labeled hypothetical inputs. I assessed this interpretation against the frozen research record; I did not independently retrieve the linked sources. Actual billable events, source mix and non-overlapping costs remain finance/business dependencies. B2B prospect cohorts must continue to be kept separate from downstream traffic/billable cohorts.

## Credible simpler alternative — preference, not a defect

For the three-day implementation, keep the required React + Python stack but use the smallest local execution shape: one FastAPI process serves built React, SQLite stores inbox/conversations/authorization/outbox/audit, one serialized worker drains a due queue, and an injected clock plus explicit scheduler tick makes demonstrations deterministic. The inference adapter can begin as a small validated template implementation with a single approved provider option, without implementing generalized routing, caches, economics optimization or any factory engine. A02–A06 remain complete architecture contracts until a selected additional increment is tested.

This is essentially a deliberately narrow implementation of the chosen architecture. It saves delivery time and makes restart/race evidence easier to interpret, at the cost of no shared deployment or scaling evidence. Coda/Dify might be simpler only if a verified existing installation already satisfies the same authority/recovery contract; its availability is not established and replacing the mandated prototype stack is not justified. Change the local approach only when measured workload or an approved integration exposes a concrete unmet need.
